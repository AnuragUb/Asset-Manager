## 3. Database Schema Highlights
The production database (`database_v2.db`) is structured to support complex auditing and historical tracking:

- **`assets`**: Stores the current state of all physical and quantity-based items.
- **`quantity_events`**: An immutable audit log of every quantity change, split, or adjustment.
- **`project_assets` & `project_history`**: Tracks the movement of assets through different project lifecycles.
- **`audit_log`**: Captures every administrative action for security and compliance.
